The lakehouse has two flows. The storage flow on top has only three tiers: bronze keeps raw events from Kafka as append-only Parquet; silver keeps cleaned data; gold keeps the tables served to the web, the dashboard and the A/B test. The compute flow below is made of Spark jobs: bronze_to_silver deduplicates, filters bots, assigns weights and writes silver; the feature step builds the interaction matrix with a time-based train and test split and time decay, plus item statistics and co-occurrence; finally ALS training, cold start, re-ranking and blending, and the result is written to gold. The band of numbers shows the data passing through each step.
