Week five of the specification asks for a schema change. We added price and star rating to silver with ALTER TABLE, filled them with MERGE, and built model version 2, which keeps recommendations inside the user’s usual price range. At equal hyper-parameters version 2 is better on every metric, by about two percent — small next to re-ranking. The first nightly run kept the old model for a design reason we explain on the operations slide; after the fix the gate compares like with like: on 11 November the scheduled run, woken by our own test clicks on the web page, retrained and deployed version 2 without anyone touching it.
