A large part of the work was operations: thirty-three issues, each with a regression test. Four examples. A path-MTU black hole on the home network silently dropped large packets. Cancelling a Spark application through the Master did not stop its client-mode driver, and the zombie collided with the new stream on the checkpoint. Our nightly deployment gate could never open, because the nightly grid did not contain the served hyper-parameters. And small files and small memory forced repartitioning and careful scheduling.
