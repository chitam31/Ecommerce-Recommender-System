Group A sees ALS only, group B ALS with thirty percent trending items. Real visitors are too few, so impressions are simulated with a position-dependent click model and labelled as simulated. Over 350 thousand impressions, B has a significantly lower click-through rate, p equals 0.015, and no difference in conversion. Treating the user as the unit, a bootstrap over users still gives p equals 0.029. The conclusion holds only under the assumptions of the simulation, which gives trending items no bonus. The live dashboard, shown in the demo, also counts the 230 real web impressions logged since 11 November, which is why its p-value is slightly different, about 0.014.
