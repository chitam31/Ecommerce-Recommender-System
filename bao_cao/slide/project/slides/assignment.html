The assignment has five objectives. O1 is the data platform: events arrive through Kafka and are stored in a lakehouse on HDFS with Delta Lake. O2 is the model: ALS for implicit feedback, a tuning grid, two baselines and cold start. O3 is streaming: a trending block that reacts within ninety seconds. O4 is an A/B experiment, and O5 covers operations: Airflow, the web application and reproducibility.
