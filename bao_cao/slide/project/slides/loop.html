These two criteria were measured on 11 November through the web application’s API. Three add-to-cart clicks reached bronze about ten seconds after Kafka received them, and the trending table fifty-one seconds after the clicks: twenty seconds of trigger interval, the micro-batch and the polling. During the first attempt a stream had died; after the restart the earlier clicks were still counted, which shows exactly-once recovery from the checkpoint.
