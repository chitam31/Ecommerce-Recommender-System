NDCG grows monotonically with rank and with alpha. With implicit feedback a large alpha gives observed pairs, especially purchases, much more weight than the empty cells, so the model extracts more signal from very sparse data. The optimum lies on the edge of the grid, so the natural next step is rank 100 and alpha 80. We also checked the obvious objection, that the grid was chosen on the test month: choosing on the first fifteen days and reporting on the last sixteen selects exactly the same configuration, with an optimism of 0.0002.
