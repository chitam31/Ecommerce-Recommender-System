Figure 3. The five Airflow DAGs and what each orchestrates in the system.

BD03 · E-commerce Product Recommender System · CS411V Big Data

8 / 28

Airflow processes no data itself; it schedules and calls spark-submit. DAG 1 runs at 2 a.m.: the main batch pipeline from bronze to the recommendation lists in gold. DAG 2 checks the trending stream every 15 minutes and restarts it if it died or went stale. DAG 3 reads the impression log every hour, computes CTR, conversion and the p-value and writes the A/B result. DAG 4 runs OPTIMIZE, Z-ORDER and VACUUM at 4 a.m. DAG 5 is triggered by hand and uses time travel to retrain on the exact old data version and compare with history. The Affects row of each box shows which tables the DAG touches.
