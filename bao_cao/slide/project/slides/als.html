We use ALS in the implicit-feedback form of Hu, Koren and Volinsky. A click is not a rating: it tells us about confidence, not preference. Every cell of the matrix enters the loss: observed pairs with preference one and a confidence that grows with alpha times the decayed score, unseen pairs with preference zero. Alternating least squares makes each half-step a set of independent least-squares problems, which Spark distributes. We tuned all 27 combinations.
